# Clicker Modes

Based on [Clicker output options](https://github.com/lgreitis/osos-lab/commit/565dfe0935ba065c8ef495e65876e5b217a087e6) by alexballvideos-netizen and lgreitis in RepriseOS.

Settings > Clicker cycles Off, Speaker, Headphones and Both instead of Off and On. Apple's click engine already routes all four values of its Clicker preference; the extension only gives the Settings row the two it lacks and the right labels. The preview picture is Apple's On picture for every mode except Off.

Enabled by default. It holds no memory beyond its image and stores nothing of its own: the mode is Apple's preference.

Disabled, or in the stock OS, Speaker and Headphones keep routing the click. The stock row shows them with Apple's swapped labels ("Speaker" for Headphones) and picks the picture by the speaker alone; Select there goes to Both.
